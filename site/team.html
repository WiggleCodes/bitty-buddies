Meet the team

Bitty Buddies is a small, independent project. Here's who's behind it.

Wiggly

Founder and developer

Hi, I'm Wiggly, the person who makes Bitty Buddies. I'm an IT engineer and developer from the UK, and I build things like this in my spare time because I love tiny pixel art and little projects that make people smile.

Bitty Buddies started with one simple idea: a few small pets that potter along the bottom of your screen while you work, to keep you company without getting in the way. Since then it's grown into packs of critters, builds and adventurers, with more on the way. Every buddy is drawn pixel by pixel, and I read every suggestion that comes in.

If you've got an idea for a new buddy, found a bug or just want to say hi, come and find me on the Discord.
