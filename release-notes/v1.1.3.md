## 🐾 Bitty Buddies 1.1.3

### ✨ New
- **Faster updates:** while Bitty Buddies is open it now checks for updates every 30 minutes, and again whenever your PC wakes up or is unlocked. New versions show up within about half an hour instead of hours.
- **No more nagging:** once an update has downloaded you're asked once. Pick *Later* and the tray menu shows **🎉 Restart to update** until you're ready (it also installs next time you quit).

### 🛠️ Fixed (since 1.1.1)
- **Pick up and throw your buddies again!** On some Windows PCs clicks went straight through the pets. The app now keeps track of your cursor itself, so grabbing and flinging works everywhere.
- **Restored licence keys work again.** If a key is restored after being revoked, its pack unlocks again on its own. No need to re-enter it.
- **Quicker licence checks:** changes to keys reach the app straight away instead of a few minutes later.
