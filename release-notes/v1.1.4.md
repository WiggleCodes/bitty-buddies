## 🪟 Bitty Buddies 1.1.4

### ✨ New
- **Window tugging!** With *Mischief* on, your buddies now sometimes leap up, grab the bottom of the window you're using and drag it along the screen. 😈 Want to see it right now? Tray menu → **Play** → **Grab a window!**
  - They leave maximised and full-screen windows alone, and let go straight away if you move the window yourself.
  - Not a fan? Untick **Mischief** in the Play menu.

### 🛠️ Fixed
- **"Move things around!" works again.** Pets used to try to grab each other at the same moment and cancel out, so nothing happened. Now they take turns, and if there's nothing to carry they'll throw in a ball to play with.
